Where each one actually wins

Every tool has a job. Here's what each is actually for.

None of these are bad tools — they're just built for a different job than running the inventory side of a resale business.

Depop & Poshmark
Built-in buyer audiences and a mobile-first listing flow make them great for discovery — but neither tracks cost, profit, or anything sold outside their own app, and both take a meaningful cut (10%–20%) of every sale.
Whatnot
Live video selling drives real urgency for collector audiences, but it requires streaming to sell and has no static listings or inventory system between shows.
eBay Seller Hub
Massive audience and solid basic reporting — but it's eBay-only, listing is slow without AI, and it has no idea what an item actually cost you. KrugerDB complements it rather than replacing it.
Vendoo & List Perfectly
Solid for blasting one listing to many marketplaces at once — but at $29–$99/month, with no AI listing, no in-person selling, and no per-item profit tracking.
Spreadsheets & DIY Airtable
Free, flexible, and fully yours — which is exactly why KrugerDB is built on Airtable, not against it. What's missing is the selling workflow: no photos, no AI, no Market Mode, no eBay sync.
StockX
Authentication builds real buyer trust for sneakers and streetwear — but it's a narrow category, charges ~9.5% in fees, and offers no inventory management at all.
